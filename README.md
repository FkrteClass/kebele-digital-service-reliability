# Factors Affecting the Reliability of Digital Public Services and Customer Satisfaction: A Study of Kebele Service Delivery

## 1. Project Overview

This research investigates the technical, human, and organizational factors affecting the reliability of digital public services in a selected kebele office in Ethiopia.

It also examines how digital service interruptions, waiting times, repeated visits, and problem-handling practices relate to customer satisfaction.

The study will use a mixed-method approach, combining quantitative and qualitative data collected through questionnaires, semi-structured interviews, and non-participant observation.

## 5. Datasets

The research is expected to use both primary and secondary data.

**Primary Data**
- Questionnaire responses from approximately 20–30 service users.
- Semi-structured interviews with approximately 5–8 employees.
- Non-participant observation records, where permission is granted.

**Secondary Data**
- Relevant scholarly articles and previous research studies.
- Available institutional documents, subject to permission and access.

Datasets will be added to the repository after data collection and anonymization.

## 6. Research Methodology

- **Research Approach:** Mixed-method approach.
- **Research Design:** Cross-sectional research design.
- **Sampling Techniques:** Convenience sampling for service users and purposive sampling for employees.
- **Data Collection Methods:** Questionnaires, semi-structured interviews, and non-participant observation.

## 7. Analysis and Reproduction Instructions

The research will use the following planned analysis methods:

**Quantitative Analysis**
- Frequencies and percentages.
- Means where appropriate.
- Correlation analysis, where the collected data and sample size permit.

**Qualitative Analysis**
- Reviewing interview transcripts and observation notes.
- Coding collected information.
- Grouping codes into relevant themes.
- Identifying patterns related to technical, human, and organizational factors.

**Reproducing the Analysis**

Instructions for reproducing the analysis will be provided after the datasets and analysis procedures have been established. These will include the required software, dataset locations, and steps for reproducing the results.
